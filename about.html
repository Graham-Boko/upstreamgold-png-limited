Corporate Identity

UPSTREAM GOLD (PNG) LTD is a nationally owned company operating in Papua New Guinea with business interest in the mining of mineral ore. The company was incorporated on the 18th October 2024 under the Companies Act 1997.

We aim to be a strategic leader in the extraction industry and a strategic joint venture partner across PNG.

Our business operations are structured into four key tiers:

01 Upstream Commercial Services
02 Upstream Petroleum & Mining Exploration Services
03 Upstream Logistics
04 Development Services

Company History

Although Upstream Gold Ltd is new to the Mining & Petroleum industry in PNG, our team comprises highly experienced geoscientists and development practitioners with collective experience in the extractive industry, both in-country and internationally.

Our key people have been involved in exploration, discoveries and development of mine fields globally as well as locally. The method of operations for each prospect depends on the key relevant drivers that will trigger the start-up.

As a strategic body corporate, we are governed by a Board of Directors who guide our Vision and Mission.

Vision

To promote, enhance and advance Service Delivery through a dynamic development models for growth & prosperity.

Mission

To create and establish strategically positioned businesses, socially responsible programs and practical solutions that will lay the foundations towards the betterment.

Core Values

  • Transparency
  • Accountability
  • Team Work
  • Innovation

Objectives

  • To use natural resources as a catalyst for development in the remote areas of PNG through improved living standards of people.
  • To increase the access of quality individuals to gain better career opportunities.
  • To increase the opportunities of individuals to obtain better training and experiences for poverty reduction and sustainable livelihoods.
