✦ WHO WE ARE

ABOUT US

Upstream Gold (PNG) Ltd is a 100% Papua New Guinean‑owned company, established in 2024. Our leadership team brings together geoscientists, development practitioners, and former government executives with over 100 years of combined experience in major national projects.

✦ Company Overview

Upstream Gold (PNG) Ltd is a nationally owned company engaged in mining, petroleum, logistics and development services. With a strong vision for sustainable development, the company aims to be a strategic leader and trusted partner in Papua New Guinea’s extractive industry.

Our experienced team combines local knowledge with global expertise to deliver long-term value for our shareholders, partners and the communities in which we operate.
