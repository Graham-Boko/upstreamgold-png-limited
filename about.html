About Us

We are a 100% Papua New Guinean-owned company, established in 2024. Our leadership team brings together geoscientists, development practitioners, and former government executives with over 100 years of combined experience in major national projects.

COMPANY OVERVIEW

We are a nationally owned company engaged in mining, petroleum, logistics and development services. With a strong vision for sustainable development, we aim to be a strategic leader and trusted partner in Papua New Guinea’s extractive industry.

Our experienced team combines local knowledge with global expertise to deliver long-term value for our shareholders, partners and the communities in which we operate.

Our Mission

To create and establish strategically positioned businesses, socially responsible programs and practical solutions that lay the foundation for long-term development.

Our Vision

To promote, enhance and advance service delivery through dynamic development models for growth and prosperity.
